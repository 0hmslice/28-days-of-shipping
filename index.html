The promise

The day before, Tibo wrote that the team is locking in. Work is limited to simplifications, more efficiency for more usage, groundbreaking features and new models, because feedback says people want things simpler.

Then came the 28-day commitment: each day, ship one clear improvement relevant for most Codex and work users, or ship a full reset.

Read the original post on X

The rules of this calendar

  • A post opens the door. When Tibo or an OpenAI account posts that day's ship, the gift unwraps with a link to the post.
  • A silent day gets coal. If a day ends in Pacific time with no post, its gift turns to coal and the garland goes dark.
  • Day 1 is Oct 5, day 28 is Nov 1. Each day runs midnight to midnight Pacific. A full reset counts as a gift.
